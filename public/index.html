Autotune — let the controller find PID values for your kiln

The kiln heats to the target and is then switched fully on and off around it a few times. From how fast it heats, cools and overshoots the controller calculates PID values. Use an empty kiln, keep an eye on it, and expect it to take 1–3 hours. Tune near the temperature you most care about controlling (higher is more representative but slower).

Pins use BCM numbering. Changes here need a restart. Double check your wiring before changing the relay pin.
A clamp-on current transformer (CT) around one element wire, read by an ADS1115 ADC on I2C (SDA = pin 3, SCL = pin 5). It confirms the elements really draw current: a pre-fire check when a firing starts, power loss or a failed element during a firing, and a relay stuck on. Wiring and calibration: docs/current-sensor.md. Live readings are under Diagnostics.
